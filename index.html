How the encoding works

Your text (with a one-character prefix flag if it starts with a known shortened-URL domain) is handed to ggwave, which encodes it as multi-tone FSK audio protected by Reed-Solomon error-correction codes — meaning the receiver can recover the original message even if a few tones get corrupted by noise, echo, or interference, rather than requiring a perfect run like our earlier hand-rolled version did.
